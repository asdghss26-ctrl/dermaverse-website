Evidence sources:

- Contrast ratios computed with the WCAG formula and `antislop-human/contrast-check.py`
- Interaction scripts for menu, form, focus, and tap targets

Limits:

- Full-resolution screenshot review was not possible in this session. Findings rely on source, DOM structure, computed styles, and measured geometry.
- The development server shows `SAMPLE` badges because `isDev()` is true. Those badges are not treated as production output.
- If any content below was confirmed directly by the client, that finding can be closed by recording the source. The current repository does not contain that confirmation.

## Result summary

No P0 or P1 indicators were found. The page does not use the blue-purple gradient, fake terminal, three-tier pricing, trusted-by logo bar, glassmorphism stack, or dead navigation that define the loudest AI templates.

The site does have a coherent warm editorial direction. The failures are concentrated in four areas:

- P4 completion and accessibility: invisible buttons, contrast, mobile menu, form validation, missing assets
- P5 authenticity: unconfirmed portrait, placeholder content treated as final, unverifiable reviews, unsupported parking claim
- P2 template residue: generic icons, numbered eyebrows, marquee, arrow repetition
- P6 implementation: scaffold leftovers and placeholder deployment configuration

## 1. HIGH: Em dash character in copy, metadata, and labels

Rule: R-02 hard gate. Catalog reference: E-7, P2.

Evidence:

- 33 occurrences in 17 source files.
- 19 rendered occurrences across 9 routes:
  - `/` 8
  - `/about` 1
  - `/services` 2
  - `/services/acne-treatment` 1
  - `/gallery` 2
  - `/faq` 1
  - `/contact` 2
  - `/book` 1
  - `/does-not-exist` 1
- Visible examples:
  - `src/app/page.tsx:117,174,206,273,314,339`: numbered eyebrows such as `01 <U+2014> What we do`
  - `src/components/home/Hero.tsx:51`: eyebrow ending in `Adyar`
  - `src/components/layout/Header.tsx:31,67`: utility bar and home aria-label
- Metadata and non-visible examples:
  - `src/app/services/page.tsx:11,14,25,43`
  - `src/app/contact/page.tsx:10,13,126`
  - `src/app/faq/page.tsx:11,14,25`
  - `src/app/gallery/page.tsx:9,12,23,44`
  - `src/app/about/page.tsx:25`
  - `src/app/book/page.tsx:24`

Why it fails: R-02 bans the character everywhere except documentation about the rule. It appears in visible headings, body copy, metadata descriptions, alt text, and an aria-label, so this is not a cosmetic edge case.

Fix direction: replace every occurrence with a comma, colon, period, or parentheses. Search all of `src/`, including metadata and `aria-label` values.

## 2. HIGH: Secondary button is invisible on dark sections

Rule: R-25, J-1. Catalog: P4.

Evidence:

- `src/components/ui/Button.tsx:27-28`: secondary variant sets `text-ink`.
- `src/components/ui/Button.tsx:62-64`: variant styles are passed to `cn()` before `className`.
- `src/app/about/page.tsx:172-173`: `Contact Us` passes `text-cream` in `className`.
- `src/app/services/page.tsx:105-110`: `Call Clinic` passes `text-cream` in `className`.
- Measured result for both buttons: text `rgb(23, 21, 18)` on background `rgb(23, 21, 18)`, ratio `1.00:1`.

Why it fails: the label is not visible. This is the primary contact path on two dark CTA sections.

Fix direction: add an explicit on-dark secondary variant, or make the override reliable. Verify the rendered label on both pages after the change.

## 3. HIGH: Repeated contrast failures across text, inputs, and focus

Rule: R-25 and R-32. Catalog: P4.

Evidence:

| Pair | Ratio | Requirement | Where |
|------|-------|-------------|-------|
| `text-slate` `#696C80` on `sand` `#F3ECE4` | 4.42 | 4.5 normal | Home FAQ, about bio, services descriptions, FAQ intro |
| `text-accent` `#F19020` on `sand` | 2.05 | 3.0 large | `10+` stat on home |
| `text-accent` on `cream` `#FFF8F5` | 2.29 | 3.0 large | `10+` stat on about |
| `placeholder:text-bronze/60` on white | 2.32 | 4.5 normal | Booking form placeholders |
| `border-taupe` `#CCC0B5` on cream | 1.70 | 3.0 non-text | Booking form input boundaries |
| Required `*` in accent on white | 2.40 | 4.5 normal | Booking form labels |
| Focus outline accent on cream | 2.29 | 3.0 non-text | Global `:focus-visible` and Button focus styling |
| Decorative 404 `text-ink/10` on cream | 1.23 | Not exempt | `src/app/not-found.tsx:11` |

Additional notes:

- `text-slate` on `cream` passes at 4.93. The failure is specifically the `sand` pairing.
- `text-bronze` on `sand` passes at 4.82. Do not darken every warm-neutral pairing.
- The `10+` stat is implemented at `src/components/ui/DoctorCard.tsx:62`.
- The global focus ring is `src/app/globals.css:184-188`; Button focus styling is `src/components/ui/Button.tsx:59`.
- The decorative 404 is exposed to assistive technology because it lacks `aria-hidden`.

Why it fails: these pairings repeat across routes and carry body copy, key statistics, input affordances, and keyboard focus. The failures are measurable, not subjective.

Fix direction: use `slate-strong` on sand, use `accent-ink` for text on cream, darken input boundaries and placeholders, use a high-contrast focus ring, and hide or remove the decorative 404.

## 4. HIGH: Mobile menu is a broken dialog when closed and when open

Rule: R-26 and R-32. Catalog: P4.

Evidence:

- `src/components/layout/MobileMenu.tsx:30-40`: the panel is always `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`.
- Closed state is moved offscreen with `translate-x-full` at line 35. Measured rectangle: `x=390`, width `390`.
- All 9 links inside the closed panel remain focusable. The first link receives focus at `x=411`.
- Escape does not close the panel. `aria-expanded` remains `true`.
- After the 9 menu links, Tab leaves the dialog and reaches the page behind it, including `Book Your Appointment` and `Call Clinic`.
- There is no focus trap and no focus restoration to the hamburger button.

Why it fails: keyboard and screen reader users can enter an invisible menu, hear modal semantics that are false, and tab into content behind an open overlay.

Fix direction: remove the closed panel from the accessibility tree, handle Escape, trap focus while open, and restore focus to the trigger on close.

## 5. HIGH: Booking form accepts blank required fields and gives no feedback

Rule: R-26, R-27, G-6. Catalog: P4.

Evidence:

- `src/components/ui/BookingForm.tsx:74-78`: form uses `noValidate`.
- Name and phone are visually marked required at lines 82 and 99 and use `required` at lines 88 and 105.
- Submission handler at lines 37-58 builds the message and opens WhatsApp without checking either field.
- Automated test with empty name and phone opened:
  `https://wa.me/917358072111?text=...Name%3A%20%0APhone%3A%20`
- No errors were rendered. No `aria-live`, `aria-invalid`, or error text exists.
- Backend submission is still a TODO at line 60.

Why it fails: a user can send an unusable booking message, and the page gives no inline error, success, or recovery state. If the popup is blocked, there is no fallback feedback.

Fix direction: validate name and phone, prevent blank submission, show inline text errors with `aria-live`, and add a visible success or fallback state.

## 6. HIGH: Hero presents an unconfirmed doctor portrait as final

Rule: R-23 and R-38. Catalog: P5.

Evidence:

- `src/components/home/ArchCard.tsx`: previously hardcoded a doctor portrait path; it now reads `clinic.doctor.portraitUrl` and falls back to the honest placeholder.
- `src/data/clinic.ts:20-21`: `portraitUrl` is `null` and `doctor.placeholder` is `true`.
- `src/components/ui/DoctorCard.tsx:33-46`: the About page therefore shows the honest `Doctor portrait` placeholder.
- The home hero and the About page present the same doctor differently.

Why it fails: the source data says the portrait is not confirmed, but the hero treats an image as the real doctor with a final-looking alt text. That is an assumed identity asset, and the inconsistency makes the missing confirmation visible.

Fix direction: use the same honest placeholder in the hero until the client supplies a confirmed portrait. If the image is client-supplied, update the clinic data and obtain approval before asserting identity in alt text.

Rule: R-38 and R-23. Catalog: P5.

- All 6 FAQs in `src/data/faqs.ts` have `placeholder: true`.

Why it fails: production removes the only visible sample label, leaving realistic services, FAQs, and clinic descriptions that look final. The content may be correct, but the repository does not prove it, and some captions are more specific than the source data.

Fix direction: replace with verified client content before launch, or keep a visible sample label in every environment. Remove specific unsupported details such as furniture and signboard descriptions.

## 8. MEDIUM: Review proof cannot be verified

## 9. MEDIUM: "Free" parking has no source

Rule: R-38. Catalog: P5.

## 12. MEDIUM: Collapsed FAQ answers remain in the accessibility tree

Rule: R-32 and R-27. Catalog: P4.

Evidence:

- `src/components/ui/FAQAccordion.tsx:57-69`: the answer panel stays mounted.
- Closed state uses only `max-h-0` and `overflow-hidden`.
- The panel keeps `role="region"` and `aria-labelledby` when closed.
- There is no `hidden`, `aria-hidden`, or conditional rendering.

Why it fails: screen readers can still reach and announce visually collapsed answers. The accessibility state does not match the visible state described by `aria-expanded`.

Fix direction: conditionally render the answer, or add `hidden` while preserving the open animation with a wrapper.

## 13. MEDIUM: Generic icons carry no real meaning

Rule: R-04. Catalog: P2.

Evidence:

- `src/data/services.ts:80`: Gem is used for cosmetic dermatology.
- `src/data/services.ts:142`: Wind is used for hair fall.

## 20. LOW: Design direction is not written down

Rule: R-31 and R-37. Catalog: P2.

Evidence:

- No `DESIGN.md` exists in the repository.
- The direction is visible in `globals.css`, but the reasons for the palette, typography, layout, arch motif, and motion are not recorded.
- No ENERGY, RHYTHM, or MOTION dials are declared.
- The only direction reference is implicit in the prior design context.

Why it fails: anti-slop requires a one-line reason for major visual decisions. Without it, the next change cannot tell a deliberate motif from a default. This is a documentation gap, not necessarily a visual defect.

Fix direction: add a short `DESIGN.md` with the identity, palette roles, type choices, dials, and a reason for the arch, eyebrow, and marquee treatments. If the direction is not confirmed, label the build as a draft.

## Verified passes

These were checked and did not produce a finding:

- R-03 layout: no horizontal overflow at `390px` or `1440px`; text did not escape containers on the inspected routes.
- R-24 navigation: all header, footer, and mobile navigation destinations resolve to existing routes; no dead nav links.
- R-26 destinations: no dead buttons or links found. The issues above are the mobile menu behavior, form validation, and labeled map placeholders.
- R-32 baseline: skip link works; focus is visible; FAQ questions are keyboard operable; tab order follows source order. The failures are the low-contrast ring and mobile dialog behavior.
- R-28 FAQ relevance: the questions are clinic-specific, covering referrals, appointments, consultation length, parking, skin types, and booking. The content is still placeholder-flagged.
- R-05 composition: the home page is not the default hero, three cards, testimonial, FAQ, CTA stack. It uses a split hero, category strip, trust strip, doctor split, editorial standard, review row, FAQ split, and visit split.
- R-11 radius: the arch, square cards, and pill buttons form a readable radius hierarchy. Not everything is a pill.
- R-15 CTAs: labels are specific to the clinic (`Book Your Appointment`, `View all treatments`, `Read the full bio`), not generic `Learn More` or `Get Started`.
- R-16 copy: no banned hype words such as seamless, revolutionary, cutting edge, unlock, or elevate were found.
- R-29 palette: the warm neutral palette and single orange accent are coherent. The problem is contrast, not color count.
- R-34 themes: no theme toggle is shipped, so there is no broken second theme.
- R-35 build: the app is running; all inspected routes return `200`; `npm run lint` and `npx tsc --noEmit` pass on the current tree. The earlier production build also passed before this audit.
