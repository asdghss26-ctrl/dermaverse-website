# Dermverse Design Direction

> Client-confirmed direction for the forest-green, champagne-gold and warm-ivory palette.

## Identity

Dermverse should feel like a calm, contemporary dermatology practice with editorial confidence. The design pairs clinical clarity with warmth, avoiding both sterile hospital cues and luxury-spa clichés.

## Palette roles

- Warm ivory `#F7F3EA`: primary page and card background, keeping clinical content light and welcoming.
- Soft beige `#EAE4D9`: alternate light section background, adding quiet separation between content bands.
- Soft gold `#E8D8B8`: text on dark green and low-emphasis warm surfaces.
- Champagne gold `#C5A46D`: borders, dividers, icons and decorative marks. Keep it off small text so contrast remains strong.
- Deep green `#244B3C`: accessible accent text, secondary actions and dark-surface transitions.
- Forest green `#183D32`: hero, footer, treatment band and appointment calls to action.
- Text `#30291F`: headings, body copy and navigation on light surfaces.

Use champagne gold for non-text structure and decorative marks. Use deep green for accent text and links where needed. Primary actions are solid forest green with ivory labels. On dark sections, secondary actions use an ivory border and label. Keep service cards and the doctor profile on ivory or beige surfaces. On the image-led hero, the primary action is the only button, and the secondary action uses an ivory border and label. Hero text sits over a forest-green scrim that keeps it readable over photography. Body and small text must reach 4.5:1 against the image under the text. Keep the right side free of text overlay. Hero controls sit on a bottom forest-green scrim with ivory progress details.

## Typography

- Display: Bodoni Moda, regular weight, tight leading, uppercase for major headings.
- Body: Jost, light to medium weights, generous line height for clinical readability.
- Eyebrows: Jost, uppercase, small size, wide tracking. They provide editorial structure without competing with display headings.

## Direction dials

- ENERGY: 2 of 5. Calm and assured, with contrast coming from type scale rather than saturated color.
- RHYTHM: 4 of 5. Generous section spacing, alternating cream and taupe fields, and a clear content cadence.
- MOTION: 2 of 5. Restrained fade-up entrances and short color transitions. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Motif rationale

- Arch shapes frame portraits and media with a soft, human geometry that echoes a clinic entrance without relying on stock medical imagery.
- Eyebrows label sections in a quiet editorial voice, keeping hierarchy clear without decorative numbering.
