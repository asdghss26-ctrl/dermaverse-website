# Dermverse Design Direction

> Draft design documentation. The visual direction is not yet confirmed by the client.

## Identity

Dermverse should feel like a calm, contemporary dermatology practice with editorial confidence. The design pairs clinical clarity with warmth, avoiding both sterile hospital cues and luxury-spa clichés.

## Palette roles

- Cream `#fff8f5`: primary page and card background, because the practice should feel light and clean without reading as hospital white.
- Taupe soft `#efe7e0`: alternate light section background, because it separates content bands without adding a second accent.
- Taupe `#d8cbc2`: hairlines, arch fills and low-emphasis surfaces, because the warm neutral keeps the clinical content soft.
- Sand `#a18267`: borders, dividers, icons, stars and decorative marks, because a single mid-tone accent can carry structure without tinting every surface.
- Sand strong `#7a5f47`: small secondary text and links on cream or taupe, because the darker tone reaches 4.5:1 where sand does not.
- Olive `#3b3418`: dark sections, the treatments band, the top bar and the footer, because the deep green-brown grounds the page in the clinic's natural palette.
- Brown `#2d241b`: headings, body copy and navigation, because the near-black brown gives editorial contrast without a cold charcoal cast.

Use sand only for non-text structure and decorative marks. Use sand strong for small secondary text. Primary actions are solid brown with cream labels. Secondary actions are transparent with a 1.5px brown border. On olive, primary actions use a cream fill with a brown label, and secondary actions use a taupe border and label. In the header, Book Consultation is a text link with a thin sand underline, not a button. On the image-led hero, the primary action is the only button, and the secondary action uses a cream border and label. Desktop hero text sits over a left-to-right brown gradient that fades to transparent by 55 percent width. Body and small text must reach 4.5:1 against the image under the text. Keep the right side free of text overlay. Hero controls sit on a bottom brown scrim, with a cream/45 progress track and cream/70 pause border, so both stay above 3:1 over photography.

## Typography

- Display: Bodoni Moda, regular weight, tight leading, uppercase for major headings.
- Body: Jost, light to medium weights, generous line height for clinical readability.
- Eyebrows: Jost, uppercase, small size, wide tracking. They provide editorial structure without competing with display headings.

## Direction dials

- ENERGY: 2 of 5. Calm and assured, with contrast coming from type scale rather than saturated color.
- RHYTHM: 4 of 5. Generous section spacing, alternating cream and taupe fields, and a clear content cadence.
- MOTION: 2 of 5. Restrained fade-up entrances and short color transitions. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Motif rationale

- Arch shapes frame portraits and media with a soft, human geometry that echoes a clinic entrance without relying on stock medical imagery.
- Eyebrows label sections in a quiet editorial voice, keeping hierarchy clear without decorative numbering.
